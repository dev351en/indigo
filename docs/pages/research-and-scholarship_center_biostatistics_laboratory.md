> The Biostatistics Laboratory (located within the Department of Health Studies) consists of a group of PhD and MS statisticians whose primary focus is on applied and collaborative work.

Its members have a broad range of experience and interests, and conduct methodological work in addition to working with researchers throughout the University. Members also participate in training and mentoring young investigators and serve on various committees and participate in the academic life of the University.
