> The Center for Italian Opera Studies (CIAO), with offices at the University of Chicago, is the home of editorial work on the critical edition of Gioachino Rossini&rsquo;s music.

> The Center also provides research and editorial support for the critical edition of the Works of Giuseppe Verdi, published by the University of Chicago Press, and for other projects in 19th-century Italian opera.&nbsp;
