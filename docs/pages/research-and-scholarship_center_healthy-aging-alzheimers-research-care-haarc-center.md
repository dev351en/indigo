### Editorial & Body Copy

> The University of Chicago Healthy Aging &amp; Alzheimer's Research Care (HAARC) Center seeks to serve as an aging and dementia research hub dedicated to the discovery of factors that promote resilience, resistance, and increased healthspan through multidisciplinary research, community engagement, education, and the development of new evidence-based interventions.&nbsp;
