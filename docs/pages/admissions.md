### Editorial & Body Copy

> We are committed to educating extraordinary students from all over the globe and all walks of life—regardless of their background or financial ability.

> The College is devoted to advancing knowledge and encouraging the habits of critical reflection, rigorous discussion, and uninhibited debate through its renowned Core curriculum and small, discussion-style classes.

> With more than 100 programs across our divisions and professional schools, students discover firsthand the power of ideas to make a difference in the world.

> UChicago offers non-degree programs spanning business, public policy, and a wide range of professional and liberal arts.

> UChicago is an intellectual destination that welcomes students from over 100 countries as part of our diverse community.

> UChicago offers a rich array of summer learning opportunities for current students, visiting college students, accomplished high school students, teachers, and others.

> Providing students with robust financial and lifelong career support while simplifying the application and financial aid processes.

> Providing a loan-free education to students from lower-income families or who are the first in their families to attend college.

> Providing support to first-generation, rural and underrepresented students and those who serve our country and communities.
