### Editorial & Body Copy

> We are continuing to build and improve our list of Accolades. Please contact us with suggested additions or corrections.

> The&nbsp;National Humanities Medal&nbsp;honors individuals or groups whose work has deepened the nation&rsquo;s understanding of the humanities, broadened citizens&rsquo; engagement with the humanities, or helped preserve and expand Americans&rsquo; access to important resources in the humanities. The award, given by the&nbsp;National Endowment for the Humanities, was established in 1988 as the Charles Frankel Prize. In 1997 it was renamed the National Humanities Medal.
