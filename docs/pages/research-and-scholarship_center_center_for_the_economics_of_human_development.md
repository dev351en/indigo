### Editorial & Body Copy

> The Center for the Economics of Human Development (Social Sciences Division), directed by Professor Heckman, encompasses multiple research initiatives which conduct rigorous empirical research to determine effective human capital policies and program design.

> These initiatives include: the Human Capital and Economic Opportunity Global Working Group and the Pritzker Consortium on Early Childhood Development (Harris School).
