> The Center for Disciplinary Innovation (CDI) is a place for pedagogical collaboration and innovation at the graduate level, complementing both the undergraduate Big Problems curriculum and the graduate offerings of the departments in the Humanities.

The CDI offers team-taught courses at the graduate level with participating faculty from different disciplines.
