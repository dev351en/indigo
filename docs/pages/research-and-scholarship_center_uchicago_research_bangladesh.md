### Editorial & Body Copy

> UChicago Research Bangladesh (URB) is an international non-profit research organization affiliated with the University of Chicago, USA.

> URB conducts research, training, and community service activities for the improvement of human health in Bangladesh and beyond. URB also works closely with scientists, health professionals and policy makers from partner institutions in Bangladesh, the USA, and other countries.
