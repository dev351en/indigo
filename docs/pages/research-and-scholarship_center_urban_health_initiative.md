### Editorial & Body Copy

> The Urban Health Initiative (UHI) is the University of Chicago Medical Center&rsquo;s long-term commitment to improving health and access to quality care for the South Side of Chicago through patient care, community-based research and medical education.

> UHI ia working to establish strong, lasting relationships with civic leaders, community organizations, health care providers and residents to develop a comprehensive health care system that is rooted in collaboration.
