> The&nbsp;National Medal of Science&nbsp;is the nation&rsquo;s highest scientific honor. The award, established by Congress in 1959 and bestowed by the President, initially recognized outstanding contributions in the physical, biological, mathematical or engineering sciences. In 1980, Congress expanded the award to include the social and behavioral sciences.

> Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar
