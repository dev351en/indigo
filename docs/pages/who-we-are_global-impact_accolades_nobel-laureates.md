### Editorial & Body Copy

> We are continuing to build and improve our list of Accolades. Please contact us with suggested additions or corrections.

> Nobel Prizes have been awarded to 102 scholars who were UChicago faculty members, students, or researchers at some point in their careers. Some of the Nobel winners whose work is closely associated with the University are&nbsp;Milton Friedman&nbsp;(Economics, 1976),&nbsp;Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar&nbsp;(Physics, 1983),&nbsp;Saul Bellow&nbsp;(Literature, 1976),&nbsp;Charles Huggins&nbsp;(Physiology or Medicine, 1966), and&nbsp;Willard Libby&nbsp;(Chemistry, 1960). The University of Chicago&rsquo;s first Nobel Laureate was&nbsp;Albert A. Michelson. The first American to win the Nobel Prize in any of the sciences, Michelson was recognized in 1907 for his measurements of the speed of light.&nbsp;Robert A. Millikan&nbsp;(Physics, 1923) did both of his prize-winning experiments on campus in the Ryerson Laboratory.

> *&nbsp;University of Chicago alumnus
&Dagger;&nbsp;Current member of faculty
