### Editorial & Body Copy

> The Center in Paris is the University of Chicago's research and teaching arm in Europe, an intellectual community of faculty, graduate and undergraduate students representing our traditionally strong ties to European thought and culture.

> The Center is located in the 13th arrondissement, between the newly expanded campus of the University of Paris VII and the French National Library.
