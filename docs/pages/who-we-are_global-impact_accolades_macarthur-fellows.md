> We are continuing to build and improve our list of Accolades. Please contact us with suggested additions or corrections.

> The MacArthur Fellows Program&nbsp;awards unrestricted fellowships to talented individuals who have shown extraordinary originality and dedication in their creative pursuits, and a marked capacity for self-direction. Sometimes known as &ldquo;genius grants,&rdquo; these&nbsp;fellowships&nbsp;are awarded for exceptional creativity, promise for important future advances, and potential for the fellowship to facilitate new work.
