### Editorial & Body Copy

> We welcome the brightest minds and boldest thinkers of tomorrow. Students seek out UChicago because they love ideas, value free expression and open debate, and are attracted to its vibrant  campus in a great city. That’s why we provide support so cost isn’t a barrier to a UChicago education—one that launches graduates into careers of consequence and lives of purpose.

> Beginning in Autumn Quarter 2027, students from families earning up to $250,000 will receive free tuition, while families earning $125,000 or less will have tuition, housing, meals, and fees covered. By substantially reducing the cost of attendance, this initiative allows more students to plan for college with clarity and make the most of all that a UChicago education offers.

> The University of Chicago is proud to sponsor a learning environment characterized by intellectual curiosity, ambition, and rigor, to shape the next generation of great thinkers whose ideas will benefit the American people and the broader world. By deepening our commitment to affordability, we are helping to ensure that the brightest minds can join us.

> We’re committed to reducing the barriers rural students and families face—through admissions programming on-campus and in local communities, and mentoring before and throughout college. Students from 416 rural counties across all 50 states are part of our community; 15% of the Class of 2030 came from rural or small-town high schools, and 740 students have joined our Emerging Rural Leaders program.

> UChicago believes a first-generation student&#x27;s path to—and through—college should be met with the same resources, mentorship, and community as anyone else&#x27;s. That&#x27;s why every first-gen student is guaranteed a First Phoenix Scholarship of at least $20,000, a paid internship, and ongoing mentorship and peer networking throughout their academic journey.

> UChicago is proud to support the men and women who have served our country. Our financial aid officers are dedicated to helping veterans, and their families, navigate every resource and opportunity available to them. The graduating Class of 2026 achieved 100% post-graduation placements in both employment and graduate study.*
