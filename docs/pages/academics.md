### Editorial & Body Copy

> At UChicago, academic inquiry is at the heart of everything we do. Our rigorous curriculum, innovative research opportunities, and vibrant intellectual community empower students and scholars to challenge conventional thought and explore groundbreaking ideas.

> At UChicago, students do much more than acquire knowledge: They develop the skills necessary to challenge ideas and assumptions and prepare for meaningful, rewarding careers. UChicago offers degree and academic programs in the undergraduate College, four divisions and seven professional schools.

> Through the breadth of its renowned Core curriculum and its nearly 70 majors and 60 minors, UChicago offers a diverse range of opportunities to undergraduates.

> Offering more than 100 graduate degree programs, UChicago provides students with flexibility through full-time, part-time, executive, and online formats.

> Education should not be intended to make people comfortable; it is meant to make them think.

> Habits of mind that are foundational to life begin well before college. UChicago operates a number of schools on Chicago&#x27;s South Side, including the renowned Laboratory Schools and three charter school campuses.
