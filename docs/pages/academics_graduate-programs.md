### Editorial & Body Copy

> Graduate education is integral to the University of Chicago&rsquo;s mission. Today, more than 100 graduate programs are available at UChicago, offering students a transformative educational experience while preparing them to make an impact in their careers&mdash;in areas ranging from academia to industry to government and nonprofits.

> Learn more about the diverse degree programs at UChicago, which offer students flexibility through full-time, part-time, executive, and online formats:

> At the University of Chicago, we believe the boldest leaders are those who never stop questioning. Our master’s programs, spanning fields from finance to the digital humanities, are aimed at students who are “endlessly curious”—eager to learn from world-renowned faculty and help shape our world.

> UChicago’s 40-plus master’s programs prepare students to tackle pressing global issues and become leaders in nonprofits, government, academia, and industry.

> For those looking to continue their academic journey or pivot their careers, UChicago’s 60-plus programs offer opportunities to conduct research in cutting-edge labs and facilities.

> This program is not only a platform for me to equip myself with technical skills, but also a place for me to actually think about how I can benefit the community or society with what I learn.

> Are you interested in non-degree programs or individual courses? UChicago provides many advanced learning opportunities spanning business, public policy, and a wide range of professional fields and liberal arts.
