### Editorial & Body Copy

> The Rustandy Center for Social Sector Innovation is the destination for people committed to helping solve complex social and environmental problems.

> As Chicago Booth's social impact hub, we build on the school's grounding in business fundamentals with experiential learning and research-based insights. Then, through programs, including the John Edwardson, '72, Social New Venture Challenge and training for nonprofit board members, we equip our community with the knowledge and tools to positively impact humanity.&nbsp;
