> Created in 1966 and directed by Jenny Trinitapoli, Associate Professor of Sociology, the Center for International Social Science Research (CISSR) sponsors and coordinates a wide variety of activities related to global and international topics.

> CISSR sponsors supports a wide range of conferences, lectures, invited speakers, and other events on international topics aimed at both university and broader audiences.
