### Editorial & Body Copy

> As an institution that values rigorous inquiry, we recognize that developing the habits of mind that are foundational to a well-lived life begin long before college. That&#x27;s why we operate N–12 schools on Chicago&#x27;s South Side

> The Laboratory Schools are home to the youngest members of the University of Chicago&rsquo;s academic community. For nearly 125 years, the Laboratory Schools have ignited and nurtured scholarship, curiosity, and creativity in students from nursery to 12th grade in a manner that emulates the core values of the University.

> The University operates three public charter school campuses within the Chicago Public Schools system, serving nearly 1,500 students on Chicago&rsquo;s South Side. Together, the three campuses provide a pre-K through 12th-grade education aimed at cultivating students to become critical thinkers who are prepared for and succeed in four-year colleges and beyond.

> Cease conceiving of education as mere preparation for later life, and make it the full meaning of the present life.
