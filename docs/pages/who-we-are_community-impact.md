### Editorial & Body Copy

> The neighborhoods where we study, work, and live are an integral part of life at the University. This is our community—and we are dedicated to fostering meaningful social change and enhancing the quality of life for all of our neighbors.

> We work with people and organizations in our community to create opportunities for local residents, small businesses, and nonprofits. To do this, we draw upon our strengths in education, research, and innovation as well as our role as the largest employer on the South Side.

> As a partner in the South Side’s storied arts and cultural heritage, we work to provide opportunities, resources, and programs that reflect our community, while lifting up local artists.

> The University provides broad, capacity-building support for community-based nonprofits and civic leaders.

> The University and UChicago Medicine are committed to promoting health equity and access to care on the South Side so that all residents have the opportunity to thrive.

> We create job opportunities for local residents,  support small businesses through workshops and industry connections, while also helping local entrepreneurs address challenges and develop new strategies for growth.

> We demonstrate our commitment to urban education through programs and initiatives that serve pre-K through high school and foster college readiness, while partnering with educators to strengthen our city’s schools.

> The University and UChicago Medicine work with community partners to reduce violence and address its root causes, build safer communities, and help those affected by violence.
