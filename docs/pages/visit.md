### Editorial & Body Copy

> Welcome to UChicago’s vibrant campus, located in the historic Hyde Park neighborhood, just miles from downtown and Lake Michigan. Explore the University’s botanic gardens, modern Gothic architecture, public art, and cultural institutions. Choose from guided in-person tours or flexible virtual and self-guided options to get a glimpse into the vibrant campus life and rigorous academic experience.

> Experience our world-class campus, historic buildings, and vibrant Hyde Park home. Discover what makes UChicago a unique place to start your college journey.

> Discover how our collaborative environment can advance your graduate studies. Explore campus and see what makes UChicago a leading destination for ambitious scholars.

> Explore the Quads, experience the Gothic architecture, and get a sense of the &#x27;Life of the Mind&#x27; from wherever you are with our virtual campus tour.

> The University&rsquo;s field-defining scholarship and world-changing ideas are reflected in the innovative architecture of the Hyde Park campus. Conceived by forward-thinking designers including Frank Lloyd Wright, Henry Ives Cobb, the Olmsted Brothers, Ricardo Legorreta, and others, the campus showcases a breadth of architectural styles from original Gothic building to modernist, brutalist, and contemporary additions.

> UChicago is one of only a few universities where the campus itself has been designated as a botanic garden. From Botany Pond to the Main Quadrangles, various gardens have been introduced through the years throughout the 217-acre Hyde Park campus, where nature complements stunning architecture.

> If you walk onto the historic quads, you feel a seriousness of purpose about the place that comes from the architecture and the landscape.

> These campus destinations are open to the public and offer a window into UChicago's art and intellectual life.

> The Institute for the Study of Ancient Cultures
Founded in 1919, the Institute for the Study of Ancient Cultures (ISAC) is a leading research center for the study of the ancient civilizations of West Asia and North Africa. With more than 350,000 artifacts, the ISAC Museum invites visitors to learn about the beginnings of our lives as humans together.

> Founded in 1974, UChicago&rsquo;s fine arts museum encourages visitors to examine complex issues through the lens of art objects and artistic practice. Within the museum&rsquo;s collection are more than 17,000 artworks from across the globe, spanning 5,000 years of history.

> Winner of the 2022 Regional Theatre Tony Award, Court Theatre is the professional theatre-in-residence at UChicago. Court Theatre reimagines classic works to speak to contemporary life, offering intimate, thought-provoking productions that invite visitors to explore the enduring questions that confront humanity and connect people.

> The Logan Center for the Arts is a place to experience, create, and contemplate art, connecting the University of Chicago with artists and communities on the South Side and throughout the city. Explore events, exhibitions, and programs open to all.

> An urban destination with a rich history, Hyde Park is steeped in intellectual tradition and home to a diverse and welcoming community.

> Discover popular places to visit near the Hyde Park campus.

> Chicago is served by two major airports: Midway International Airport and O&rsquo;Hare International Airport. Midway is closer to the Hyde Park campus, but both offer ground transportation options to the University of Chicago.
