### Editorial & Body Copy

> The University of Chicago opened a major new academic center in Delhi in 2014, supporting and expanding opportunities for collaboration among scholars and students from India and Chicago, across academic disciplines.

> The Center in Delhi is a home for research and education for University of Chicago faculty, graduate students, and undergraduates working in India and throughout South Asia, as well as Indian researchers and students representing a wide array of institutions, and scholars from around the world.
