### Editorial & Body Copy

> We are continuing to build and improve our list of Accolades. Please contact us with suggested additions or corrections.

> The Wayne C. Booth Graduate Student Prize for Excellence in Teaching was established in 1991 in honor of Wayne C. Booth, the George M. Pullman Distinguished Service Professor in English Language &amp; Literature and the College. Students and faculty members submit nominations, and the winners each receive a $2,000 cash award. The awards parallel the&nbsp;Llewellyn John and Harriet Manchester Quantrell Awards for Excellence in Undergraduate Teaching, which recognize distinguished teachers on the faculty.
