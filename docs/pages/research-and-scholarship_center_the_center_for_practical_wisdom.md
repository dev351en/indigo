### Editorial & Body Copy

> The mission of the Center for Practical Wisdom is to deepen the scientific understanding of wisdom and its role in the decisions and choices that affect everyday life.

> The Center for Practical Wisdom will connect scientists, scholars, educators, and students at the University of Chicago with researchers and scholars internationally who are interested in studying and understanding wisdom, and it will provide guidance and support for learning about wisdom research, initiate new wisdom research, and disseminate the findings of such research.&nbsp;
