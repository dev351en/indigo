### Editorial & Body Copy

> We are continuing to build and improve our list of Institutes and Centers. If you have a suggested addition or correction, please let us know.

> Established in 2006 and named for Herrlee G. Creel (1905-1994), the Creel Center supports the study of early Chinese textual materials, including especially materials that have been unearthed in China.

> Already in his classic study The Birth of China (London: Jonathan Cape, 1936), Professor Creel pioneered the use of oracle-bone inscriptions to study the cultural history of the Shang civilization centered on the site of Anyang. Later, in his The Origins of Statecraft in China: The Western Chou Empire (Chicago: The University of Chicago Press, 1970), Creel made thorough use of bronze inscriptions to describe the administration of the Western Zhou government. With the incredible discoveries of unearthed texts of all sorts over the last decades, the center hopes that the Creel Center will extend Professor Creel&rsquo;s interest to subsequent generations. Most of its programs will necessarily be based at the University of Chicago, but will also strive to make them available to scholars and students throughout the world.
