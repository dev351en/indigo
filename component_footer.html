The University of Chicago is a community of scholars dedicated to research, academic excellence, and the pursuit and cultivation of learning. As part of this commitment, UChicago does not discriminate in admissions, employment, or participation in programs and activities on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity or expression, national or ethnic origin, shared ancestry, age, status as an individual with a disability, protected military or veteran status, genetic information, or other protected classes under the law.
