Research and Scholarship

Ideas that shape our world

UChicago research, fueled by the freedom to challenge assumptions and follow ideas where they lead, defines new fields of study and helps solve age-old problems. From medicine to quantum, and economics to climate policy, UChicago scholars and students are making foundational advances in research and achieving breakthroughs that benefit our world.

Research empowers discovery

Through their foundational research, UChicago scholars and students create knowledge in their fields and enable new areas for discovery.

Measuring the earliest light in the universe

Using the South Pole Telescope, UChicago scientists studied the cosmic microwave background to map the afterglow of the big bang.

Learn more

Exploring how the humanities can advance AI research

UChicago scholars are examining the opportunities of generative AI models provide across disciplines—and what they can reveal about knowledge and creativity.

Learn more

Understanding how children’s language forms 

When does a child start building words rather than parroting them? UChicago linguistics, psychologists and computational scientists sought to find out.

Learn more

Explaining how brain cells connect

UChicago scientists built a simple model to examine how brain cells organize and cluster together in a variety of organisms.

Learn more

Research improves our health

From advancing cancer immunotherapy to developing new treatments that target disease at the molecular level—our medical breakthroughs are impacting lives.

Inventing a pacemaker powered by light

UChicago researchers have created a featherlight, wireless implant that uses light to regulate heart or nerve activity, potentially replacing pacemakers.

Learn more

Harnessing the body’s cancer-fighting powers

UChicago scientists are using cancer immunotherapy to treat patients for leukemia and lymphoma.

Learn more

Tackling research on disease

Leveraging unique opportunities to work in UChicago labs, College students drive innovation in fields including neuroscience, bioengineering and cancer therapy.

Learn More

Recycling and reusing fresh water

UChicago scientists are leading a ‘moonshot’ initiative to remove dangerous chemicals from the Great Lakes. 

 
Learn more

Unlocking the secrets of the gut microbiome

UChicago researchers are working to understand the role that bacteria in our gut play in our health—and how they may impact diseases and allergies.

Learn more

Research tackles complex social problems

Breakthrough scholarship at UChicago has not only changed the way we think and view the world, but has benefitted society in fundamental ways.

Addressing complex problems in cities and schools

The UChicago Urban Labs use academic research to inform effective policy-making to help limit crime and improve education across the country.

Learn more

Measuring air pollution’s impact on life expectancy

UChicago research in Asia and Africa found the risk to our health is comparable to smoking

Learn more

Understanding how parents’ decisions shapes their kids

UChicago scholars have measured the impact of choices within families—from reading at bedtime to showing affection to allowing screentime.

 
Learn more

Examining the benefits and risks of climate systems engineering

UChicago scholars are researching the science and policy implications of climate systems engineering, to better understand the technology while educating students and society about a warming planet.

Learn more

Measuring how preschool benefits generations of families

Nobel Prize-winning economist James Heckman’s decades of research found the benefits of early education on social mobility and poverty. 

Learn more

Research drives technological advances

Discovery at UChicago is actively focused on solving problems, while developing and employing new techniques and innovative technology.

Advancing work toward a secure quantum internet

A 124-mile quantum network built in Chicago is one of the country’s first testbeds for quantum security technology.

Learn more

Restoring touch to people with prosthetics

By placing tiny electrodes in parts of the brain responsible for moving and feeling, UChicago scientists have created more responsive prosthetic limbs.

Learn more

Designing the next generation of batteries

Engineers are developing batteries that eliminate 'forever chemicals,' such as PFAS, by using safer materials and new manufacturing techniques.
Learn more

Developing tools to protect against AI piracy

Software developed by UChicago computer scientists deliver a ‘poison pill’ to generative AI tools to protest artists' work from plagiarism.

Learn more

From Idea to Industry

We turn groundbreaking research into new businesses. UChicago has helped entrepreneurs take big ideas—from new cancer treatments to green energy solutions—from the labs to the real world. Through the Polsky Center’s world-renowned New Venture Challenge, more than 370 startups have been launched, including Grubhub, Braintree/Venmo and Simple Mills.

Research that made history

Discover the fascinating stories and ‘eureka’ moments behind big, world-changing ideas at UChicago—and the people behind them.

Redefined the field of economics
From Milton Friedman’s ideas on monetary policy to Gary Becker’s theory of human capital to Richard Thaler’s founding of the field of behavioral economics, UChicago scholars have transformed economic research. 
Learn more
Unlocked the secrets of black holes
UChicago scientists have conducted groundbreaking research on black holes, beginning in 1930 when 19-year-old Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar proposed the controversial idea that stars collapsed at the end of their lives—research that led to the discovery of black holes.
Learn more
Established the country’s first sleep lab
For the past century, UChicago have pioneered the study of sleep—from discovering REM sleep to unraveling sleep’s impact on our health.
Learn more

Our economic impact

UChicago faculty have played an influential role in shaping the U.S. economy, through their insights on financial policy or their work as economic advisors to presidents. Their breakthroughs in medicine have led to new cures, startups and industry partnerships—and their research in partnership with Argonne and Fermilab national laboratories have large-scale economic impact. Studies have shown that every $1 spent by the NIH generates $2.56 of economic activity.
