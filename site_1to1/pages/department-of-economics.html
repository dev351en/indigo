The Birthplace of the Chicago School: Rigorous Empirical and Theoretical Inquiry

The Kenneth C. Griffin Department of Economics at the University of Chicago is world-renowned for defining modern economic thought. With 30+ Nobel laureates affiliated as faculty, students, or researchers, the 'Chicago School' revolutionized price theory, human capital, monetary policy, behavioral economics, and empirical microeconometrics.

Department Highlights

  • 30+ Nobel Laureates in Economic Sciences affiliated with UChicago
  • Pioneered modern Price Theory, Rational Expectations, and Experimental Economics
  • Home to the Becker Friedman Institute for Economics (BFI) and the Center for the Economics of Human Development
  • State-of-the-art academic home in the beautifully restored historic Saieh Hall for Economics

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Kenneth C. Griffin Economics

The Undergraduate Program in Economics is one of the most prestigious in the world, offering both standard and empirical/data-intensive tracks. Students learn foundational price theory, econometrics, and game theory directly from Nobel laureates and leading economic researchers.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Master’s & Professional Degree Curricula

The Department offers joint-degree options, including the Master of Arts Program in the Social Sciences (MAPSS) with Economics concentration, and dual credentials with Chicago Booth and the Harris School of Public Policy.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Ph.D. in Economics at UChicago is an intensive, internationally celebrated program producing scholars who shape academic institutions, central banks, and international policymaking. Students receive full multi-year financial fellowship packages.

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Price Theory & Applied Microeconomics

Foundational price determination, industrial organization, market design, and competitive equilibrium.

Macroeconomics & Monetary Policy

Search and matching models, macroeconomic dynamics, financial frictions, central banking, and international finance.

Econometrics & Quantitative Methods

Non-parametric econometrics, treatment effect evaluation, high-dimensional estimation, and machine learning in economic models.

Human Capital & Development

Early childhood intervention, education economics, labor market mobility, and inequality dynamics pioneered by James Heckman.

Behavioral & Experimental Economics

Field experiments, social preferences, behavioral nudges, and public goods mechanisms pioneered by John List and Richard Thaler.

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • Saieh Hall for Economics state-of-the-art lecture auditoriums and research suites
  • Becker Friedman Institute for Economics (BFI) convening spaces
  • Center for the Economics of Human Development (CEHD) research laboratories
  • Experimental Economics Observation and Behavioral Lab
