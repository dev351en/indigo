Theoretical Rigor, Empirical Power, and Field-Defining International Scholarship

The Department of Political Science at the University of Chicago is one of the most distinguished centers of political scholarship in the world. Known for fostering groundbreaking approaches in international relations realism, democratic accountability, political theory, and racial politics, our faculty address the foundational questions of power, governance, and human society.

Department Highlights

  • Home to leading political theorists and international relations realists including John Mearsheimer and Robert Pape
  • Pioneered the Chicago Center on Democracy and the Chicago Project on Security and Threats (CPOST)
  • Deep integration across quantitative empirical methods, formal political economy, and political philosophy
  • Outstanding placement record for doctoral graduates in top academic and policy institutions worldwide

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

International Relations & Grand Strategy

Offensive realism, great power competition, nuclear deterrence, international security, and war dynamics.

Comparative Politics & Democratization

Democratic backsliding, clientelism, authoritarian regimes, political institutions, and electoral accountability.

Political Theory

Democratic theory, republicanism, feminist political theory, historical political philosophy, and critical theory.

American Politics & Political Behavior

Racial politics, public opinion, congressional behavior, party polarization, and voting rights.
