Empowering Leaders with The Chicago Approach to Business Education

Founded in 1898, the University of Chicago Booth School of Business is the second-oldest business school in the world and the proud home of 10 Nobel laureates in Economic Sciences. Known worldwide for 'The Chicago Approach'—a rigorous, discipline-based education grounded in fundamental principles of economics, statistics, and behavioral science—Chicago Booth shapes the global business landscape.

Department Highlights

  • 10 Nobel Laureates in Economic Sciences affiliated with Chicago Booth faculty
  • Consistently ranked among the top MBA programs in the world
  • Historic Charles M. Harper Center designed by world-renowned architect Rafael Viñoly
  • Global campuses in Chicago (Hyde Park and Downtown), London, and Hong Kong

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Chicago Booth School of Business

The Trott Business Program and the Booth Undergraduate Business Studies curriculum offer College students rigorous foundational coursework in finance, accounting, and marketing.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Master’s & Professional Degree Curricula

Full-Time MBA, Evening MBA, Weekend MBA, and Executive MBA (XP) programs delivering transformative leadership development.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Ph.D. in Business is the first doctoral business program founded in the United States, offering specialized tracks in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Marketing, and Behavioral Science with full financial support.

Guaranteed 5-Year Doctoral Fellowship Package:
  • 100% Full Tuition Remission covered by the University
  • Competitive Annual Living Stipend ($44,000+ per year)
  • Comprehensive UChicago Student Health Insurance (U-SHIP)
  • Guaranteed research assistantship and travel conference grants

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Finance & Efficient Capital Markets

Asset pricing, corporate finance, financial derivatives, banking fragility, and empirical investment research.

Behavioral Science & Decision Research

Behavioral economics, decision heuristics, choice architecture (Nudge), and consumer psychology.

Accounting & Corporate Governance

Financial statement analysis, auditing standards, executive compensation, and disclosure economics.

Entrepreneurship & Social Innovation

Venture capital, startup acceleration, private equity, and impact investing (Polsky Center and Rustandy Center).

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • Charles M. Harper Center state-of-the-art tiered auditoriums and trading simulation labs
  • Polsky Center for Entrepreneurship and Innovation
  • Rustandy Center for Social Sector Innovation
  • Fama-Miller Center for Research in Finance
