Evidence-Based Policy: Data, Economics, and Real-World Impact

The Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago approaches policymaking through the lens of data science and microeconomic analysis. Housed in the LEED Platinum Keller Center, Harris faculty and students tackle global climate policy, urban education, crime reduction, municipal finance, and international governance.

Department Highlights

  • LEED Platinum Keller Center designed by Farr Associates and Studio Gang
  • Home to Nobel Laureate in Economic Sciences Prof. James A. Robinson (2024)
  • World-renowned Urban Labs: Crime Lab, Education Lab, Health Lab, Poverty Lab
  • Pioneering degrees in Computational Analysis and Public Policy (MSCAPP)

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Harris School of Public Policy

The Public Policy Studies major in the College gives undergraduates rigorous training in policy design, econometrics, and program evaluation.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Master’s & Professional Degree Curricula

Master of Public Policy (MPP), MS in Computational Analysis and Public Policy (MSCAPP), and Evening Master's credentials.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Ph.D. in Public Policy trains researchers in quantitative policy evaluation, political economy, and applied microeconomics with complete 5-year fellowship funding.

Guaranteed 5-Year Doctoral Fellowship Package:
  • 100% Full Tuition Remission covered by the University
  • Competitive Annual Living Stipend ($44,000+ per year)
  • Comprehensive UChicago Student Health Insurance (U-SHIP)
  • Guaranteed research assistantship and travel conference grants

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Urban Policy & Crime Mitigation

Evidence-based violence intervention, police reform, urban schooling, and youth development (Urban Labs).

Political Economy & Governance

Institutional development, authoritarian politics, conflict studies, and voting behavior.

Energy & Environmental Policy

Carbon pricing, global climate negotiations, energy access in developing nations, and renewable transitions.

Child Development & Education Policy

Early childhood interventions, parental investments, school choice mechanisms, and social mobility.

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • The Keller Center Forum and collaborative policy design suites
  • University of Chicago Urban Labs research command center
  • Energy Policy Institute at Chicago (EPIC)
  • Center for Municipal Finance research laboratories
