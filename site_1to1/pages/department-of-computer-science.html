Pioneering the Foundations of Computing, AI, Systems, and Quantum Information

The Department of Computer Science at the University of Chicago is an epicenter for cutting-edge computing research. Situated in the John Crerar Science Building, our department bridges foundational theoretical computer science with high-impact systems, human-computer interaction, privacy, trustworthy AI, and quantum architectures in close collaboration with Argonne National Laboratory and the Chicago Quantum Exchange.

Department Highlights

  • Home to the Data Science Institute (DSI) and the Chicago Quantum Exchange
  • Pioneered foundational research in computer security, cloud systems, and privacy (e.g. Fawkes, Nightshade)
  • World-leading faculty in quantum computer architecture and machine learning theory
  • Comprehensive undergraduate, Master's (MPCS), and Ph.D. degree curricula with full doctoral fellowship support

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Computer Science

The Undergraduate Program in Computer Science offers B.S. and B.A. degrees, providing a rigorous grounding in algorithms, programming languages, computer systems, and theoretical foundations, alongside advanced electives in machine learning, graphics, cryptography, and human-computer interaction.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Master’s & Professional Degree Curricula

The Master of Science in Computer Science (MPCS) and MS in Computational Analysis and Public Policy (MSCAPP) offer tailored, career-defining curricula for emerging software leaders, systems engineers, and computational policymakers.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Computer Science prepares scholars for leadership in academia and industrial research. Doctoral students conduct groundbreaking research from quarter one, supported by comprehensive 5-year funding covering tuition, stipend, and medical insurance.

Guaranteed 5-Year Doctoral Fellowship Package:
  • 100% Full Tuition Remission covered by the University
  • Competitive Annual Living Stipend ($44,000+ per year)
  • Comprehensive UChicago Student Health Insurance (U-SHIP)
  • Guaranteed research assistantship and travel conference grants

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Foundations of deep learning, trustworthy AI, computer vision, natural language processing, and multi-agent coordination.

Systems, Networks & Architecture

Cloud computing, storage architectures, scalable distributed computing, distributed tracing, and resilient hardware design.

Quantum Computing & Information

Quantum software, architectural co-design, error mitigation, and quantum networking in partnership with the Chicago Quantum Exchange.

Security, Privacy & Society

Adversarial machine learning, biometric privacy protection, usable security, cryptography, and surveillance mitigation.

Theory & Algorithms

Computational complexity, spectral graph theory, approximation algorithms, quantum algorithms, and algorithmic game theory.

Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)

Haptic computing, wearable devices, interactive AI interfaces, accessible technologies, and digital fabrication.

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • John Crerar Science Building high-performance compute clusters and GPU farms
  • Center for Data and Computing (CDAC) discovery incubators
  • Chicago Quantum Exchange testbeds and cryogenic laboratories
  • Human-Computer Integration Lab & Actuated Experience Research Lab
