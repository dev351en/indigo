The Premier Law School for Socratic Inquiry and Law & Economics

The University of Chicago Law School offers a rigorous, interdisciplinary legal education characterized by unyielding commitment to free inquiry, the Socratic method, and empirical scholarship. As the birthplace of Law and Economics, Chicago Law trains visionary attorneys, judges, legal scholars, and public servants.

Department Highlights

  • Consistently ranked among the top 3 law schools in the nation
  • Birthplace and global capital of the Law and Economics movement
  • Historic landmark Eero Saarinen-designed Law School building
  • Nationally renowned faculty in Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence, First Amendment, and Antitrust

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in The University of Chicago Law School

The Law, Letters, and Society major in the College offers undergraduates interdisciplinary training in legal institutions and political philosophy.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Law & Economics

Economic analysis of legal rules, antitrust law, corporate governance, bankruptcy, and regulation.

Constitutional Law & Free Expression

First Amendment jurisprudence, originalism, separation of powers, and the Free Speech Century.

Jurisprudence & Legal Philosophy

Philosophical foundations of law, the capabilities approach, justice theories, and legal realism.

Criminal Justice & Empirical Legal Studies

Sentencing reform, judicial behavior, criminal procedure, and data-driven legal analytics.
