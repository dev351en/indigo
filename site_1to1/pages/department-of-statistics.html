Inferential Rigor, Statistical Machine Learning, and Big Data Foundations

The Department of Statistics at the University of Chicago is a global leader in foundational statistical theory, high-dimensional data analysis, statistical machine learning, biostatistics, and statistical genetics. Situated in Jones Laboratory, the department drives the theoretical engines behind modern data science in collaboration with the Data Science Institute.

Department Highlights

  • Pioneered foundational generalized linear models (Peter McCullagh) and history of statistics (Stephen Stigler)
  • MacArthur Fellowship awardees and leaders in distribution-free predictive inference and conformal prediction
  • Joint degree programs in Data Science, Machine Learning, and Computational Finance
  • Close partnerships with the Data Science Institute, Argonne National Laboratory, and Biological Sciences Division

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Statistics & Data Science

Undergraduate B.A. and B.S. degrees in Statistics and Data Science providing deep training in probability, mathematical statistics, computational modeling, and modern machine learning.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Master’s & Professional Degree Curricula

The Master of Science in Statistics (MSS) and MS in Financial Mathematics offer intensive computational and theoretical training for top data science, quantitative finance, and doctoral careers.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Ph.D. in Statistics trains scholars who develop new mathematical frameworks and statistical methodologies for high-dimensional inference, bioinformatics, and machine learning, supported by comprehensive multi-year funding.

Guaranteed 5-Year Doctoral Fellowship Package:
  • 100% Full Tuition Remission covered by the University
  • Competitive Annual Living Stipend ($44,000+ per year)
  • Comprehensive UChicago Student Health Insurance (U-SHIP)
  • Guaranteed research assistantship and travel conference grants

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Statistical Machine Learning & AI

Conformal inference, non-convex optimization, neural network theory, and distribution-free uncertainty quantification.

High-Dimensional Inference

Large p small n estimation, sparse covariance estimation, minimax estimation, and random matrix theory.

Statistical Genetics & Genomics

Genome-wide association mapping, single-cell RNA-seq statistical models, and phylogenetic inference.

Biostatistics & Public Health Data

Survival analysis, clinical trial design, causal inference, and epidemiological Bayesian modeling.

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • Jones Laboratory computational research labs and collaborative spaces
  • Data Science Institute (DSI) high-throughput computing facilities
  • Biostatistics and Genomics Core computational clusters
