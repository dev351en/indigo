Advancing the Frontiers of Chemical Science

Founded in 1892 as one of the inaugural departments of The University of Chicago, the Department of Chemistry has pioneered fundamental breakthroughs that define modern chemistry, from radiocarbon dating to quantum simulation and bioorganic synthesis. Our faculty and alumni include 15 Nobel laureates and numerous members of the National Academy of Sciences.

The department resides primarily within the historic Searle Chemistry Laboratories and Kent Chemical Laboratory in the heart of the Hyde Park campus, providing state-of-the-art computational infrastructure, NMR facilities, mass spectrometry, and x-ray crystallography centers.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

The doctoral program in Chemistry is designed to develop independent, creative research scholars. Graduate students engage in original investigation from their very first quarter, supported by close mentorship from faculty advisors across computational, physical, organic, inorganic, and materials chemistry.

  • Guaranteed Comprehensive Funding: All doctoral candidates in good academic standing receive full tuition remissions, comprehensive university healthcare, and an annual living stipend for the duration of their Ph.D. studies.
  • Flexible Interdisciplinary Research: Graduate students have access to joint appointments, Argonne National Laboratory resources, and the Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering.
  • Matriculation & Start Terms: Ph.D. cohorts enter each Autumn Quarter (with onboarding and laboratory rotations beginning in August).
