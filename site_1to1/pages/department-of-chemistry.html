Transformative Discoveries Across Synthetic, Biological, Physical, and Theoretical Chemistry

Founded in 1892 as one of the inaugural departments of the University of Chicago, the Department of Chemistry has pioneered fundamental discoveries that define modern chemical science, including radiocarbon dating, electron transfer theory, and RNA epigenetics. Home to 15 Nobel laureates and historic Searle Laboratories.

Department Highlights

  • 15 Nobel Laureates in Chemistry affiliated with the department
  • Historic Searle Chemistry Laboratory and Kent Chemical Laboratory in Hyde Park
  • Pioneers in epitranscriptomics, bioconjugation, quantum dynamics, and inorganic nanomaterials
  • Doctoral program with complete multi-year tuition grants and living stipends

Academic Degree Programs

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Chemical Biology & Epigenetics

Reversible RNA methylation (m6A), targeted covalent modification, cellular imaging probes, and directed evolution.

Inorganic & Materials Chemistry

Colloidal semiconductor nanocrystals, molecular quantum bits, coordination networks, and electrocatalytic materials.

Physical & Ultrafast Chemistry

2D electronic spectroscopy, single-molecule biophysics, femtosecond laser dynamics, and energy transfer.

Theoretical & Computational Chemistry

Electronic structure theory, multiscale molecular dynamics, quantum mechanics/molecular mechanics (QM/MM), and density functional development.

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • Searle Chemistry Laboratory modern synthetic and analytical suites
  • Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) Facility (500-800 MHz spectrometers)
  • Mass Spectrometry & Proteomics Core
  • X-Ray Crystallography Diffraction Facility
