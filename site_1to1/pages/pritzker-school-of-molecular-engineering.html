Engineering from the Molecular Level Up to Solve Global Challenges

The Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering (PME) is the first school in the United States dedicated to molecular engineering. Founded in partnership with Argonne National Laboratory, PME integrates physical sciences, life sciences, and engineering to tackle pressing global challenges in quantum information, immunoengineering, sustainable energy, and materials design.

Department Highlights

  • First dedicated School of Molecular Engineering in the nation
  • Headquarters of the Chicago Quantum Exchange and national quantum initiatives
  • World-leading cleanrooms, cryogenics, and molecular synthesis facilities in the Eckhardt Research Center
  • Interdisciplinary faculty spanning medicine, physics, chemistry, and computational biology

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering

The B.S. in Molecular Engineering provides an innovative curriculum combining chemistry, physics, biology, and applied mathematics with concentrations in Quantum Engineering, Immunoengineering, and Chemical/Materials Engineering.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Master’s & Professional Degree Curricula

Master of Engineering (M.Eng.) programs offering specialized technical and leadership training in quantum engineering, bioengineering, and clean energy.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Ph.D. in Molecular Engineering offers an interdisciplinary doctoral curriculum where students work across traditional departmental boundaries, supported by full 5-year fellowships, competitive stipends, and research grants.

Guaranteed 5-Year Doctoral Fellowship Package:
  • 100% Full Tuition Remission covered by the University
  • Competitive Annual Living Stipend ($44,000+ per year)
  • Comprehensive UChicago Student Health Insurance (U-SHIP)
  • Guaranteed research assistantship and travel conference grants

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Quantum Engineering & Technology

Solid-state quantum bits, quantum optics, neutral atom processors, quantum internet, and cryogenic electronics.

Immunoengineering & Cancer Therapy

Antigen-specific immunotherapy, biomaterials for lymphatic drug delivery, engineered vaccines, and cellular therapeutics.

Materials for Energy & Sustainability

Next-generation solid-state batteries, electrocatalysis, self-healing polymers, and water purification membranes.

Computational & AI Molecular Design

Machine learning for molecular discovery, multi-scale simulation, free energy calculations, and de novo peptide design.

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • Soft Matter Characterization and Bioengineering Core
