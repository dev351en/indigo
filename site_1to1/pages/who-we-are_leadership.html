UChicago Leadership

The University is governed by a Board of Trustees and directed by academic and administrative leaders, including the President, Provost, Deans, and Vice Presidents. Together, they ensure the fulfillment of the University’s mission, uphold its core values, and manage its operations and academic affairs.
