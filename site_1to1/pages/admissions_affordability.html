Affordability & Outcomes

A transformative education—within reach

We welcome the brightest minds and boldest thinkers of tomorrow. Students seek out UChicago because they love ideas, value free expression and open debate, and are attracted to its vibrant campus in a great city. That’s why we provide support so cost isn’t a barrier to a UChicago education—one that launches graduates into careers of consequence and lives of purpose.

Expanding financial support for College students

Beginning in Autumn Quarter 2027, students from families earning up to $250,000 will receive free tuition, while families earning $125,000 or less will have tuition, housing, meals, and fees covered. By substantially reducing the cost of attendance, this initiative allows more students to plan for college with clarity and make the most of all that a UChicago education offers.

Rural students

We’re committed to reducing the barriers rural students and families face—through admissions programming on-campus and in local communities, and mentoring before and throughout college. Students from 416 rural counties across all 50 states are part of our community; 15% of the Class of 2030 came from rural or small-town high schools, and 740 students have joined our Emerging Rural Leaders program.

Learn more

First-generation students

UChicago believes a first-generation student's path to—and through—college should be met with the same resources, mentorship, and community as anyone else's. That's why every first-gen student is guaranteed a First Phoenix Scholarship of at least $20,000, a paid internship, and ongoing mentorship and peer networking throughout their academic journey.

Learn more

Veterans

UChicago is proud to support the men and women who have served our country. Our financial aid officers are dedicated to helping veterans, and their families, navigate every resource and opportunity available to them. The graduating Class of 2026 achieved 100% post-graduation placements in both employment and graduate study.*

Master's Degrees

A master’s degree from the University of Chicago is a lasting investment—advancing your career while building critical skills and connections with world-class peers. Funding varies by program, with many offering merit scholarships (partial to full tuition).

Learn About Master's Funding

Ph.D. Programs

Doctoral students typically receive full funding packages that cover tuition, health insurance, and an annual stipend of at least $46,350 for living and research expenses for the duration of the program. There are also a range of additional opportunities—from prestigious fellowships to internships and roles on and off campus that help you explore different career paths.

Learn More About Doctoral Funding

Additional Support in Professional Programs

  • Harris guarantees at least 60% tuition remission for students with U.S. public service experience, and offers full tuition plus a living stipend to a select cohort of exceptional students through the Dean's Scholarship.

    • Chicago Booth's Edwardson Civic Scholars Program offers full-tuition scholarships for Evening and Weekend MBA students committed to careers in the public or social sector.

      • The Law School supports students at every stage through admissions scholarships, summer public interest funding, emergency grants, postgraduate fellowships, and a Loan Repayment Assistance Program for graduates pursuing public-sector careers.

        • The Crown Family School awards tuition scholarships based on merit and financial request to approximately 95% of our master’s students. Additional merit scholarships are also available to students who have previously served in programs such as the Peace Corps, Teach For America, and AmeriCorps. Students who continue to face a financial gap after their initial award may also request scholarship reconsideration. Most scholarships are renewable for second-year students who remain in good academic standing.

          • The Pritzker School of Medicine provides generous scholarship support to a majority of students, including full-tuition scholarships for half of each entering medical school class. This support allows Pritzker students to graduate with average debt well below the national average for medical schools.* Pritzker also supports the development of physician-scientists through guaranteed summer research funding and fellowships for MD students to pursue research projects.
