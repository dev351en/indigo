Translational Medicine, Clinical Excellence, and Compassionate Care

The Pritzker School of Medicine is one of the top medical schools in the nation, uniquely co-located with the University of Chicago Medical Center and the undergraduate College on a contiguous campus. Pritzker integrates rigorous biomedical science, clinical inquiry, and health equity leadership.

Department Highlights

  • Top-ranked medical school embedded within the University of Chicago Medicine health system
  • Pioneered surgical innovations, bone marrow transplantation, and cancer chemotherapy
  • Exceptional Medical Scientist Training Program (MSTP / MD-PhD) supported by the NIH
  • Ranked #1 for student satisfaction and clinical research immersion

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Pritzker School of Medicine

Master of Science in Public Health Sciences and MS in Biomedical Informatics.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Medical Scientist Training Program (M.D./Ph.D.) provides integrated clinical and basic science doctoral training with full tuition, stipend, and research allowances.

Guaranteed 5-Year Doctoral Fellowship Package:
  • 100% Full Tuition Remission covered by the University
  • Competitive Annual Living Stipend ($44,000+ per year)
  • Comprehensive UChicago Student Health Insurance (U-SHIP)
  • Guaranteed research assistantship and travel conference grants

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Cancer Genetics & Immunotherapy

BRCA mutations, cellular therapies, tumor microenvironment targeting, and personalized oncology.

Cardiovascular Science & Arrhythmias

Molecular mechanisms of heart failure, cardiac electrophysiology, and ischemic injury prevention.

Gastroenterology & Microbiome

Inflammatory bowel disease, microbiome-host interactions, and metabolic syndromes.

Health Equity & Healthcare Quality

Physician communication, medical education reform, systemic disparities in health access, and hospital operations.

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • Center for Care and Discovery (CCD) inpatient clinical pavilions
  • Biological Sciences Learning Center (BSLC) simulation centers
  • Comprehensive Cancer Center state-of-the-art research laboratories
  • Duchossois Family Institute for Microbiome Research
