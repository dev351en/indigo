Rigorous Conceptual Inquiry into Mind, Language, Ethics, and the History of Thought

The Department of Philosophy at the University of Chicago is celebrated for its distinctive integration of historical depth and rigorous systematic philosophy. Our scholars lead the world in ancient Greek philosophy, German idealism, philosophy of mind and action, ordinary language philosophy, and contemporary ethics and political philosophy.

Department Highlights

  • Renowned faculty including Martha C. Nussbaum, Jonathan Lear, Agnes Callard, and James Conant
  • Global center for Kant, German Idealism, and the Wittgensteinian tradition
  • Close collaboration with the Law School, Department of Classics, and Committee on Social Thought
  • Vibrant undergraduate philosophic debate societies and doctoral mentorship

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Philosophy

The B.A. in Philosophy offers intensive training in critical reasoning, textual analysis, and philosophical argumentation, covering history of philosophy, logic, ethics, and metaphysics.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Ph.D. in Philosophy is a world-class doctoral program preparing researchers and teachers of philosophy, supported by comprehensive multi-year fellowships.

Guaranteed 5-Year Doctoral Fellowship Package:
  • 100% Full Tuition Remission covered by the University
  • Competitive Annual Living Stipend ($44,000+ per year)
  • Comprehensive UChicago Student Health Insurance (U-SHIP)
  • Guaranteed research assistantship and travel conference grants

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

History of Philosophy (Ancient & Modern)

Plato, Aristotle, Hellenistic philosophy, Kant, Hegel, and 19th-century European philosophy.

Philosophy of Mind & Action

Nature of agency, intentionality, self-consciousness, practical reason, and philosophical psychology.

Ethics, Political Philosophy & Law

Capabilities approach, theories of justice, moral emotions, democratic theory, and constitutional jurisprudence.

Philosophy of Language & Logic

Wittgenstein, ordinary language philosophy, truth theories, skepticism, and formal semantics.

World-Class Facilities & Affiliated Institutes

  • Stuart Hall philosophical lecture rooms and departmental library
  • Center for Law, Philosophy, and Human Values (Law School)
  • Franke Institute for the Humanities seminar spaces
