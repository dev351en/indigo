Pure Mathematical Brilliance in the Heart of Hyde Park

The Department of Mathematics at the University of Chicago is world-renowned for deep, transformative research in pure mathematics. Housed in the collegiate Gothic beauty of Eckhart Hall, our faculty and researchers have produced foundational breakthroughs in algebraic geometry, number theory, algebraic topology, geometric group theory, and partial differential equations.

Department Highlights

  • Fields Medalists including Prof. Ngô Bảo Châu and historical faculty such as Paul Cohen
  • World-leading center for the Langlands Program and arithmetic geometry
  • Historic birthplace of modern algebraic topology and Category Theory (Saunders Mac Lane)
  • Vibrant undergraduate and doctoral communities with renowned Putnam and research mentoring traditions

Academic Degree Programs

Undergraduate Program in Mathematics

The Undergraduate Program in Mathematics offers B.A. and B.S. degrees, renowned for its challenging Honors Analysis sequence and advanced courses in abstract algebra, topology, differential geometry, and complex variables.

Degrees Conferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.S.) • Administered through The College

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program

Full Fellowship Funding

The Ph.D. in Mathematics is one of the world's most sought-after programs, immersing doctoral candidates in active mathematical discovery under the mentorship of internationally prominent mathematicians, fully funded with tuition and living fellowships.

Research Areas & Specializations

Faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate researchers collaborate across interdisciplinary fields, driving theoretical innovations and empirical breakthroughs.

Algebraic Geometry & Number Theory

Automorphic forms, Galois representations, p-adic geometry, the Langlands program, and modular forms.

Algebraic & Differential Topology

Homotopy theory, operads, characteristic classes, low-dimensional manifold topology, and knot invariants.

Geometric Group Theory & Dynamics

Mapping class groups, Teichmüller theory, hyperbolic geometry, ergodic theory, and smooth dynamical systems.

Partial Differential Equations & Analysis

Dispersive equations, non-linear harmonic analysis, geometric measure theory, and fluid dynamics.

Mathematical Logic & Set Theory

Model theory, classification theory, computability, and the foundations of infinite mathematics.
